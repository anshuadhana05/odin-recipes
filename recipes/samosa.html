Samosa

TEST Home

Samosas are a popular Indian snack — crispy, golden pastries filled with spiced potatoes and peas. They're deep-fried until crunchy and typically served hot with mint or tamarind chutney.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1/4 cup oil or ghee
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
  • Water, as needed
  • 3–4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1–2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Oil, for deep frying

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, carom seeds, and oil/ghee in a bowl. Add water gradually and knead into a stiff dough. Cover and let it rest for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add grated ginger and chopped green chilies, sauté for a minute.
  4. Add mashed potatoes, peas, turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Stir in fresh coriander leaves and let the filling cool.
  6. Divide the dough into small balls and roll each into an oval shape. Cut each oval in half.
  7. Take one half, fold it into a cone shape, sealing the straight edge with a little water.
  8. Fill the cone with 1–2 tbsp of the potato filling.
  9. Seal the top edge of the cone with water, pressing firmly to close.
  10. Heat oil in a deep pan on medium heat. Fry the samosas in batches until golden brown and crispy, about 8–10 minutes per batch.
  11. Remove and drain on paper towels. Serve hot with mint or tamarind chutney.
